## Here's What we did
*TL;DR*

This was an ancillary project we did with the Axis Bank to help their users manage their co-brand credit cards. We built an SDK that would live inside the merchant apps and the users could manage their cards from there- Scapia and Airtel were among the first adopters.

The challenge was to take a large set of banking capabilities - transactions, statements, payments, card controls, limits, EMI conversions - and make them live cohesively inside the merchant app.
[VIDEO: final prototype.mp4]

## Requirement Context
*Some Overview*

A co-brand credit card(Scapia Axis Card in this case) sits between two ecosystems. The card was issued by Axis Bank, but the customer's everyday experience lived inside the Scapia App.

> Axis owned the underlying banking infrastructure. Merchants owned the surrounding product experience. An SDK was needed that could sit in between.

The long-term vision was that this SDK would eventually power credit-card experiences across multiple co-brand partners.
