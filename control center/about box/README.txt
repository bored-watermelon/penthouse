THE ABOUT BOX
=============

What the things in the box on the about section open into. Add, rename or delete files here and the site follows.
(This folder isn't a section of the grid: nothing in here shows up in work or play.)


camera photos/
  The photos on the camera, when it's opened from the box. Pictures: jpg, png, webp.
  They're shown in the order they were taken, read from the file name, the way phones name them:
      IMG-20221017-WA0002.jpg, IMG20251020183650.jpg, PXL_20251129_164755494.jpg
  A photo whose name doesn't say when it was taken goes after the dated ones, by name.


songs/
  The songs on the iPod. mp3, m4a, aac, wav, ogg, flac or opus.
  Name each one with its title and artist, and that's what the iPod shows:
      Song-[I Love You So]; Artist Name-[The Walters].mp3


sketchbook/
  photos/
    The sketchbook's pages, in file-name order. Number them to set it: 01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg ...
    - A tall or square picture gets a page of its own; a wide one gets a whole spread, across both pages.
    - To write a caption under one, put it after " - " in the file name:   07 - a cafe in kochi.jpg
    - JPG, PNG or WebP. Big files are fine; they're shrunk when the site is built.
    - Each is blended into the page like ink, so white paper around a drawing disappears into the page.
    While this folder is empty, the book borrows the Art pictures from /play. The moment there's one picture
    here, it uses only these.

  stickers/
    The stickers on the cover. Cut-outs: PNG with a see-through background, or SVG.
    Give one a size in its file name: "heart (3cm).png" is 3cm on its longer side, against a cover 9.5cm wide.
    Without one, it's 2.8cm. They're stuck on in file-name order, spread over the cover, and can be peeled off
    and moved. The ones in here now are placeholders: replace them with your own.

  cover.jpg (optional, next to photos/ and stickers/)
    A picture to use as the cover itself, instead of the plain black one.
